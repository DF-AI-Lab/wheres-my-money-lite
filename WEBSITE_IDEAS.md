## More ideas (Claude, 28 Oct)
- Payday countdown in the hero: "27 days to payday", ticking live.
- Scroll the month: as you scroll down, the days pass and bills tick green (scroll-driven animation).
- "Try your own numbers": type your pay and 3 bills, see your spare. Nothing saved, nothing sent.
- Privacy x-ray: a dotted line tries to leave the phone and stops at the edge: "Your numbers never leave."
- Pots as jars that fill up as you add spends; over = it spills (red).
- Install demo: a little animation of "Add to home screen" and the Lite icon landing on a home screen.
- Version timeline: v0.01 to today, each version a dot you can tap (what changed).
- Sound toggle (off by default): a soft click on Add, a coin on payday.
- Easter egg: tap the Lens icon 5 times and it spins and winks.
- 9:16 clips for social: Payday flash, a bill ticking green, the fingerprint wake.

## Tools to learn (research, 28 Oct)
- **Claude Motion**: Anthropic's code-made animation (sharp text, export MP4). Beta from 8 Oct 2026, **Team / Enterprise plans only**. Check your plan.
- No `/motion` command in Claude Code. Nearest: **Remotion** skills (code -> MP4) and community animation skills.
- **Higgsfield**: AI video with film camera moves. Has a Claude connector: `https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp` (needs a paid plan, uses credits).
  AI video garbles text and numbers: make the phone with a green screen, put the real app screen on top.
- **GSAP** (free now, all plugins): best for the HUD lines. **Rive**: animations that react to taps (fingerprint wake).
- Plugins in the Claude directory: Remotion, Motion Reel, video-shotcraft, AI Video Editor (ManyMotions).
- Hosting idea: website at `/`, app at `/app/` on the same GitHub Pages site (manifest scope + start_url = /app/).
