Where's My Money? Lite

Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version

Your money stays with you. The app has no server and no account, and nobody but you can see your bills or spends.

Where your data lives

  • Everything you type is saved in this browser, on this device only.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. The app never sends your money data to us or to anyone else.
  • If you clear this browser's site data, the data on this device is gone, so keep a backup.

Google Drive (only if you choose it)

  • If you tap Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to sign in. The app then asks for drive.file only: it can see just the backup file it made, never the rest of your Drive.
  • The backup is scrambled with your passcode before it leaves your device, so only you can open it.
  • It goes into your own Google Drive. We never get a copy.
  • The sign-in only lasts while the page is open. The app does not see your Google password or your email.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies for tracking.
  • We do not sell, share or look at your data. We cannot: it never reaches us.

Remove your data

  • On this device: clear this website's data in your browser settings.
  • In Google Drive: delete the file wheres-my-money-backup.json from your Drive.
  • Stop Google access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
